## Generated material maps and previews

Limestone, mortar, clay colour variants, iron, bread, fruit, soil, end-grain and leaf maps were generated procedurally. They are not scans. Glass, wax, interior shading and some metal/flower colours use supplementary PBR materials. Streak decals originate from the existing kit.

The model previews are rendered from the actual Blender assets. No font files, third-party application binaries, external character models or AI-generated bitmap images are bundled. The offline catalogue uses system fonts and has no external script dependency.
