# Surface texture provenance

These unmodified 1K JPEG PBR maps are from Poly Haven, released as CC0-1.0. They are free/public-domain assets, not proprietary UO data. Provider license: https://polyhaven.com/license ; CC0 deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The manifest records each asset's author, source page, measured physical dimensions, exact download URL, provider MD5 and verified SHA-256. Attribution is retained despite CC0 not requiring it. Albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are used; no source images were edited. Runtime materials grade/blend them for the project's stylized presentation. Download/recheck with scripts/prepare-surfaces.ps1.
