# Poly Haven models

Unmodified 1K glTF models from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released as CC0-1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). The manifest records each asset's source page, authors, exact download URL, provider MD5 and verified SHA-256. Attribution is kept although CC0 does not require it. Verify or re-download with scripts/prepare-polyhaven.ps1.
