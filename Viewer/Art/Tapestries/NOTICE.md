# Tapestry photographs

Photographs of public-domain tapestries from the Art Institute of Chicago, released under CC0-1.0 (https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images). Cropped to each hanging's proportions by scripts/add-artic-textures.py; manifest.json records each artwork page, IIIF source, crop box and SHA-256. Credit is kept although CC0 does not require it.

France, Beauvais. https://www.artic.edu/artworks/2102
