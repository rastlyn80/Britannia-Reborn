# Visual review launchers

The optional art and animation review shortcuts live here. Double-click the
relevant `.cmd`; each resolves the project and its scripts from this folder.
Normal game/server launchers remain in the repository root.

Prepared local art is required for most reviews. Sources, preparation and
validation are documented under `docs/`. Captures and generated galleries stay
in ignored `outputs/`; source/derived art stays in ignored `Viewer/local-art/`.

- `Review-Ostard.cmd` and the Forest/Frenzied/Ethereal shortcuts: original,
  geometry and material comparison.
- `Review-Equines.cmd` / `Review-Horses.cmd`: accepted horse profiles and riding.
- `Review-Mount-Riding.cmd`: integrated ostards, ki-rin, skeletal mount and llamas.
- Human, equipment and Third Dawn shortcuts: their named experiments.

Additional review entry points belong in this folder, not the root.
