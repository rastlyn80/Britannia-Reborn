# Yellow and red Dragon terrain, breath and corpse

The native Dragon constructor can roll yellow body 12 or red body 59. Both retain their original Third Dawn meshes, skins and movement. They share the existing Drake terrain and wing support while keeping the red Dragon's four fitted Enhanced actions.

The replay measured 8,680 Classic and 9,920 Enhanced full-skin poses across flat ground, slopes and a 22 cm tread. The candidate cleared the target in all sampled poses. The baseline fell below the target in 7,320 Classic and 7,578 Enhanced samples. The test exercised source walks, wing poses, breath, flight and landing. It measured 112 Classic and 144 Enhanced decoded death handoffs, including the original ground and airborne deaths, with 1,904 and 2,448 complete-skin fall samples. The largest first-pose shift was under 4 micrometres. The final frozen geometry cleared the sampled surface by about 2 mm.

The isolated server passed 45 checks per profile across both stock body rolls. Both connections observed the stock 1.3-second breath windup and fireball. The normal client launched the projectile from the animated jaw, and the native target took fire damage. Both bodies walked, ran and settled into visible static corpses. Native human carving yielded seven scales of the matching colour and 22 barbed hides. The fixture controlled the breath opportunity and target.

The original meshes, asset pins and optional actions remain in the existing creature and Enhanced-action manifests. The local terrain, death and native evidence is under `work/creatures/native-form-audit/enhanced-source-gaps/common-forms-enhanced/dragon-living-source/`.
