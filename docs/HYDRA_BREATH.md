# Hydra and Abscess fire breath

The two native constructors share body 265. Classic now plays a dedicated breath on their retained five-headed rig. The central neck rises and the jaw opens using the original idle as the base pose. Enhanced retains its fitted breath. Both modes hold the pose when Classic melee groups 4–6 or Enhanced melee type 0 arrive during the windup. Death still interrupts it.

The projectile socket is attached to the middle head. Forward+ close-ups at release confirmed that the adjusted origin sits at the open central mouth. The native body, material choices and corpse hue still follow the existing routes. The eight-check World replay passed in each client mode, including the ordinary melee after the windup and a dyed static corpse after an interrupted breath. The focused existing Enhanced World suite passed 52 checks.

In the isolated trial, the stock AI chose Fire Breath for Hydra and Abscess in both client modes. Each mode passed 18 checks. The four initial projectiles launched 1,303–1,317 ms after windup and every target took damage. The fixture positions the combatants. Wild encounters remain open. Crystal Hydra shares the body and uses the same server cue. Its separate cold damage, hue and multi-target attacks need their own live presentation trial.
