# Twisted Weald Dryads

The shipped ModernUO spawner file has entries named `DryadA` at Ilshenar (2171, 1181, −44) and `Dryada` at (2207, 1180, −36). Both names now resolve to the same subclass of ModernUO's native `MLDryad`. The mapping reuses body 266, hue zero, Mage AI, the inherited abilities and the existing body 266 model and corpse route. [Official habitat guide](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/world/dungeons/twisted-weald/) · [Pinned reference spawn data](https://api.github.com/repos/ServUO/ServUO/git/blobs/2f0e2071e20259b8adb1d98965ce2abd413b6414)

The native trial imported the shipped spawner file into a disposable Ilshenar world. Both configured spellings spawned an innocent body 266 Dryad with Mage AI. Both clients received the living bodies. Each Dryad and its spawner kept the same serial after restart. The fixture then dealt lethal damage, and both clients received matching corpses on graphic 0x2006. Twenty initial checks and twenty restart checks passed. A follow-up isolated trial set the stock-spawned Dryad’s initial target. The normal AI then activated both inherited area abilities. Both players received the peace notice and the graphic 0x376A effect, and both had an active peace timer. The ability moved the male test player’s robe and trousers out of the equipped layers while the female witness kept hers. The test observed the normal chance triggers. It did not measure their recurrence or render their complete combat sequence. The fixture replenished test-player health while waiting. There were 87 initial assertions and five after restart for login and receipt retention. The separately named Frightened Dryad and the Haven quest Dryad retain their own roles.
