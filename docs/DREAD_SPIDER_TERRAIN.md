Body 11 retains the Third Dawn mesh, eight leg chains, existing source motion links and both Classic falls. The Enhanced route retains the warm abdomen marking and all five fitted gestures. Its procedural silk opening appears during the emergence and clears at death. The frozen cadaver uses native graphic 0x2006 with body 11 and the server hue.

The geometry tests cover flat ground, slopes and a 22 cm tread. The largest adjacent vertical corrections were about 171 mm during the Classic fidget and 181 mm during Enhanced Silk Emergence on the highest tread. The initial death pose stayed within one micrometre of the displayed joints. The frozen mesh cleared its support by about 2 mm and carried no silk-opening geometry.

The stock Dread Spider kept its native hue, constructor stats and treasure level. The native melee damage hook applied lethal poison to a disposable target and the stock spider itself was immune to lethal poison. The stock Lightning spell also damaged the disposable target. Both clients received walk, run and death, and the normal client displayed the matching corpse and partial hue. The corpse held eight packed spider silks. The fixture initiated movement, spell targeting and gestures. Autonomous selection and treasure-map drops were outside this trial.
