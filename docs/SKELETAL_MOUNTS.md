# Skeletal mounts

Skeletal Mount, Hell Steed, Grizzled Mare and Red Death share native body 793 and mount item `0x3EBB`. The optional Enhanced profile uses their original Third Dawn skeletal horse. It keeps the 34-joint rig and the painted harness in the 256×256 atlas. The refined mesh has 3,432 triangles. Red Death retains its native `0x21` hue, and full and partial dyes use the server-provided hue on the living model, ridden model and corpse.

The source table and ten named original motion members are fingerprinted in the preparation script. Eat and Fidget2 have identical source payloads. Each original animation supplies 33 matched tracks. The omitted animation-only branches contain no ancestor or weighted joint from the mesh. The extra unweighted model node remains on the rig. The enhanced scene preserves all ten original clips.

The official Enhanced sequence `0xbc9dc469e5ea0386` lists thirteen groups with 670 registered frames in `AnimationFrame3.uop`. The fitted motions use the original rig for the high strike, rear cast, guard, combat idle, quiet nod, high fidget, alert and two different falls. Four groups use the original gait and recoil clips. The native Classic five-frame Chaos Breath selection uses its own 1.67-second windup, with the stock projectile leaving the animated muzzle.

Run `python -B scripts/prepare-skeletal-mount.py --models "<private Third Dawn Models directory>" --install`. The recipe generates the scenes in `work/creatures/skeletal-mount-bake` and installs the packed Enhanced scene and its SHA-256 manifest under `Viewer/local-art/mounts`. The source archive data, sprites and generated scenes remain private. Use `--check-installed` to verify the installed scene.

The two-client native tests passed 175 checks across both rider rigs and all four constructors. They exercised stock mounting, movement, dismount, hues, death and the bridle found only on Red Death’s corpse. The three stock Chaos Breath users passed 55 further checks, with controlled targeting and native damage timers. The two client modes passed 5 Classic and 49 Enhanced route checks. Both riders passed 600 fitting checks across 108 poses, and the 39 Forward+ renders cover all thirteen Enhanced groups. The finite-plane tests measured 1,701 living poses and 27 corpse transfers, including both fitted falls and the original Classic fall. The independent rebuild matched the skinned mesh, original atlas, joints and all 905 saved animation tracks.
