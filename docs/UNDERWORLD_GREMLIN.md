# Underworld Gremlin

The shipped Underworld spawner at Ter Mur (1108, 1153, −22) resolves Gremlin as body 724 with base hue 0 and the stock ArcherAI. The server equips a bow and 60–80 arrows and packs five apples. The isolated two-client trial passed 29 checks before restart and 15 after restart. Both clients received the autonomous native arrow graphic 0x0f42 from six tiles away. They also received the original body and hue on corpse graphic 0x2006. One Lucky Coin was observed in a sample of twenty fixture kills. The spawner and mobile serials survived restart. Archery skill 65.3 follows the reference Tactics value, and the six-tile engagement range makes the stock archer usable with the normal bow.

No Gremlin mesh was present in the scanned Third Dawn table. The fitted model reuses that client's rigged Orc (body 17), with narrower proportions, a slate and beige atlas, and a pelvis-skinned tail. Original, rounded and modern model treatments are generated from the installed donor. The source frame archive has 18 groups with 800 frames. The Enhanced action sequence is 0xbf2468bda704ed47. Enhanced type 11 selects the overhead lunge at group 12, and the flight flag selects group 19 with combat action fallback.

Classic and Enhanced World replay passed 56 and 578 checks, respectively, including neutral and dyed bodies, both falls, and a frozen corpse for each. Grounded terrain tests sampled 6,510 full-skin poses across flat, sloped and stepped supports. The modern appearance was rendered in 60 poses for six clips. Native bow fire was observed through the two clients' decoded projectile events. The Classic and Enhanced replays equipped the Third Dawn bow through native layer 2, applied the item hue independently, and sent the decoded arrow from the drawn string. The weapon is put away on equipment removal or death. Ratman archery passed 45 regression checks in each mode. The Gremlin bow uses the existing local bow preparation script (scripts/prepare-centaur-bow.py), which pins model 649 and its atlas. The body can still load before that optional art is prepared.

Rebuild with `python scripts/prepare-later-creatures.py --body 724 --classic <owned-classic-install> --sequence <reviewed-AnimationSequence.uop>`. The source takes hashes from scripts/later-creature-inputs.json. The locally rebuilt scenes and original art stay out of Git.
