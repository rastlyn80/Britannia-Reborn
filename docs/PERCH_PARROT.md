# Perch parrot

The Mondain's Legacy client table maps body 282 to its own Parrot model. The wild parrot uses body 831 and retains its separate Third Dawn model. The recovered perch bird has 180 weighted source vertices, 276 triangles and 42 joints. The mesh contains the atlas UVs and weights for both wings. Two additional unweighted wing objects have no UV buffers. The adapter verifies their object and triangle tables before excluding their geometry, while retaining both wing joints. The extracted idle, walk and two fidgets all bind to the original rig. The original 256-pixel atlas is retained and the modern mesh has 4,416 triangles. The perch-scale fit uses a 33 cm bird height. Combat and death use the shared compatibility motions because the cabinet only supplies the four named clips.

Rebuild with `scripts/prepare-later-creatures.py --body 282 --legacy-source <extracted-owned-directory> --classic <owned-classic-install> --sequence <reviewed-AnimationSequence.uop>`. Body 282 currently validates the Mondain's Legacy members against their recorded hashes. The Classic and Enhanced archive paths are shared arguments for the other builds. Extract the mesh, four motions and atlas under the exact `legacy_members` names in `scripts/later-creature-inputs.json`, along with the cabinet model table. The three game-derived scenes stay local.

The three treatments passed 300 sampled poses across the four original clips on a flat support plane. Both client profiles passed 20 checks for the distinct body, its normal idle, its two fidgets, three native hues and matching static corpses. The replay uses decoded test packets.

The expanded isolated ModernUO trial passed 44 initial and 22 restart checks. A visitor was refused placement and renaming. The owner placed the dyed bird on a stock perch, and the registered co-owner renamed it and received the refusal for an occupied perch. Both clients received the correct body and hue, the feeding response and echoed nearby speech. Feeding used one wafer from a stack. The trial checked the registered cooking recipe and used a supplied stack to test the normal drag and drop packets. The same frozen bird survived save and restart. Chopping the perch returned a deed and removed the old bird. Placing that deed restored a single dyed bird with the same name. The test used 24 native deaths per boss and obtained the reward from 18 Lady Melisande and 2 Crystal Hydra corpses.

The two isolated live World runs passed 52 initial and 35 restart checks in Classic mode and 49 initial and 35 restart checks in Enhanced mode. Native packets created the original rig and hue, kept it in the animal idle, and built graphic 0x2FF4 as the wooden perch when tile definitions were absent. The rendered perch's upper bound and the bird's native anchor agreed within 3 mm at initial placement, after restart and after redeeding. The horizontal anchors also agreed within 3 mm. The live runs use the normal World node tree headlessly and extend its local terrain bounds around the disposable house.

A further isolated run passed 75 initial and 22 restart checks. A registered co-owner placed their own dyed parrot on an empty perch through the normal item target. Two clients received the bird. A player then opened the Cooking menu with a skillet, selected Preparations and crafted a wafer. The native recipe consumed the dough, honey and ten raw fish steaks. The fixture added one wafer to the resulting stack to test that feeding consumed a single wafer.

Forward+ captures showed the need to resolve the weighted body through its own 25-entry joint palette. The two helper objects each have a separate one-joint palette. The recovered tail extends below the feet, so the live perch neutralizes terrain corrections on the model and pins the midpoint of the original toe joints to the crossbar. All 300 sampled source poses passed again. The greatest flat-plane penetration of the source scenes was 0.030 mm. With the corrected scenes and toe grip, the normal live viewer passed 121 Classic and 121 Enhanced checks. The measured toe midpoint stayed within 3 mm at placement, restart and redeeding. The local review image uses the Enhanced profile. The combined creature extension compiled with zero warnings and passed 75 initial and 22 restart checks using the same normal gameplay packets.
