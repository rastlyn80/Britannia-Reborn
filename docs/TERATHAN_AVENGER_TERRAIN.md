# Terathan Avenger on slopes and steps

Body 152 keeps its own Third Dawn mesh and 57-joint rig. The original pincer attacks and fitted gestures stay on the same skeleton in both profiles. The terrain treatment shared with the Drone and Warrior measures the Avenger's own skin. Both Classic fall packet groups and the five fitted Enhanced gestures were tested through the displayed fall and the frozen static corpse.

The maximum first-pose movement was under one micrometre and the static corpse settled about 2 mm above the support. The adjacent visual correction reached about 227 mm during a high-step attack sample and remains visible.

Both clients observed stock movement, hue and the matching body-152 corpse. The native melee hook applied Deadly poison to the disposable target. The stock immunity rejected Deadly poison, and the opposition rule identified the Ophidian as an enemy. A human carver retrieved two ribs.
