# Orc action expansion in occurrence order

Ordinary orcs rank second in the configured Trammel occurrence audit. Four existing Third Dawn forms now have distinct overhead and sweeping melee strikes, a casting gesture, and a ground-slap/pillaging category. Their original meshes, UVs, rigs, source clips and upgraded local atlases remain in use. The preserved source attack supplies the first strike. These are authored modern replacements, not recovered original UO choreography.

The pinned native `BaseWeapon.PlaySwingAnimation` randomly selects classic groups 4-6 for monster melee swings. Previously the two additional groups reused the first strike. The new attacks have separate windup, impact and recovery paths, verified against each other and the preserved source strike on every actual imported rig. Classic casting 12 and ground-slap 11 now have explicit clips. The shaman's ground-slap deliberately reuses its recovered `Stomp`; the other three forms use a measured crouch/reach.

Additional modern packet categories are explicit per-model metadata: 2 -> Hit, 5 -> Fidget, 14 -> Pillage. The pinned ClassicUO reference maps these monster categories to hit groups 15/16, fidget 17/18 and ground-slap 11. `CreatureVisual` reads the optional mapping; records without it retain their existing routes. The action ledger includes these model-specific entries. The pinned server's ordinary monster melee path uses classic packets; modern category broadcasts are exercised by the isolated fixture, not claimed as proof of new stock-AI behaviour.

The source rigs need two particular treatments. Their thighs branch from the spine, so a torso gesture also needs leg compensation to keep the feet planted. Animation files omit grasp/control leaves present in the body skeleton; the authoring helper retains those bind leaves and their parent links. Hands and fingers keep their original local pose relative to the forearm. Holding palms fixed in world space produced sharp wrist bends in initial close-ups; the final gesture follows the forearm and preserves the prop grip in hand space.

Implementation and provenance:

- `scripts/creature-humanoid-actions.py`: measured two-bone reach, anatomical shoulder-derived forward direction, distributed torso bends, crouch and foot anchoring. Orc-lord underscore spelling is handled without renaming the source skeleton.
- `scripts/prepare-creatures.py`: four explicit source forms receive the additional clips and packet-category metadata. Local preparation records pin the helper hash and retain every source member/atlas/prompt hash.
- `Viewer/Presentation/CreatureGestureTests.gd`: actual imported skin, hand-path separation, source wrist/prop offsets, native request routing and flat/sloped collision-plane checks, with Forward+ captures. Inputs are fingerprinted before the sweep and checked unchanged afterwards.
- `scripts/accept-creatures.py`: requires exact passing gesture, asset/import, recipe and runtime receipts before accepting these forms. `scripts/test-creatures.ps1` runs the gate automatically for orcs.
- `scripts/test-creature-humanoid-actions.py`: seven synthetic checks for rotated-ancestor IK, reach limits, spine-parented legs, endpoint continuity, distinct attacks, attachment leaves and explicit source reuse.
