# Wyvern, ordinary rat and BullFrog terrain

Wyvern (62) keeps the recovered Third Dawn mesh, source action keys and four optional Enhanced motion groups. Terrain support uses the two original three-joint wing chains. The stock flight and landing clips retain their original local poses and lift over higher treads. The default Daemon retains its previous support dimensions and thresholds.

The former Wyvern landing intersected the test slope by 1.824 m. The terrain-aware renderer passed 3,100 Classic and 4,650 Enhanced full-skin poses, plus 56 and 72 decoded falls. The steepest test tread still causes up to 221 mm of lift between adjacent fidget samples. Normal World packet replays covered the two winged bodies, Classic recoil, flight and landing, Enhanced gestures, and static corpses in both packet orders. Native Wyvern trials passed 52 Classic and 58 Enhanced checks with two isolated clients. The genuine melee hook applied Deadly Poison to an Ogre, both clients received the poison indicator, and the native timer lowered the target's health. The stock Wyvern refused its own poison and its corpse retained its Lesser Poison Potion.

Rat and Sewer Rat (238) keep the shared br-n518 CC0 four-paw rig, existing Classic actions and the locally prepared Enhanced fits documented in [Ordinary Rat Enhanced actions](ORDINARY_RAT_ENHANCED.md). The renderer validates the public death scene against its published provenance and validates the optional local scene through its prepared asset hash. The original renderer allowed roughly 120 mm of overlap at the 12 cm test tread. The terrain renderer passed 2,790 Classic and 4,960 Enhanced full-skin poses, plus 56 Classic and 80 Enhanced decoded falls. The largest measured first-pose movement was 0.507 mm. The static geometry keeps the body, hue and pose. The highest tread still causes a visible lift of about 60 mm during the run.

Native Rat and Sewer Rat passed 95 Classic and 123 Enhanced checks across four class and owner combinations per profile. The trials verify distinct constructor stats, normal movement, stock taming and weapon actions, full and partial hues, and the final visible corpse in the loaded Britain region. A server run now interrupts the Classic fidget immediately. The Enhanced selectors also replay the prepared optional motions.

BullFrog (81) retains its distinct [Third Dawn mesh and Giant Toad motion link](FROGS.md). The native model keeps its original hop, four source limbs and tongue. Its previous pose overlapped the high tread by 221 mm. Both client profiles now clear all 2,480 measured living poses and all 56 decoded death handoffs. The largest first fall displacement is 0.508 mm. The highest tread can still lift some frames by about 220 mm. Both client profiles passed 53 isolated checks each, including constructor hue, stock Animal Taming, feeding one raw rib through the native creature interaction, the original tongue action, and genuine corpse carving for five hides and one rib with the human bonus.

Reproduce the terrain and corpse handoffs with `scripts/verify.ps1` and the `test-source-wyvern`, `test-source-rat` and `test-source-frog` terrain and fall replays. The local fitted art requires its original inputs and stays outside Git. The rat's public mesh, rig and licence remain available from the normal project checkout.
