## Grounding correction

The large-animal solver's 2.5–16 cm stance window flattened the source zombie's approximately 5.6 cm walking swing to below 1 mm in a measured modifier pass. Six running/walking stance-window cases also lost more than 15 mm of genuine lift. The opt-in source-biped preset instead releases feet above 3 cm and plants feet near the sampled surface below each foot. Slopes are measured against that surface, rather than the actor's centre height. Joint rotations preserve the measured leg segment lengths; native entity coordinates stay unchanged.

Support is confined to the measured single-surface, four-influence source biped. Other accepted families retain their configured support. The actual avatar is created through CreatureRegistry, with explicit hunched head/camera metadata; it is not merely a free-standing mesh test.

Godot restores the original animation pose after applying modifiers to the rendered skin. The focused test captures corrected soles and leg lengths at [`SkeletonModifier3D.modification_processed`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_skeletonmodifier3d.html#signals), so the terrain assertions use the corrected poses seen by the renderer. Earlier probe results taken outside that phase are diagnostic only.

## Reproduction and queue

Run `python -B scripts/prepare-creature-kind.py walking_dead_zombie` against the pinned local archive. The selector preserves accepted family forms, archives previous preparation metadata under `work/creatures/preparation-history/`, and prepares only requested source meshes; it does not activate unrelated ghoul/mummy/rotting-corpse models. Its live guard test refused a subset of accepted deer without changing that family's preparation bytes.

`scripts/test-creatures.ps1 -Families @('walking')` imports, validates source skins, runs the actual-avatar/terrain/hue/corpse gate, renders the standard views and accepts only matching exact inputs. The acceptance step checks the selector hash, selected kind set, final support evidence, asset/import/preparation hashes and critical runtime files. Then run `scripts/test-horses.ps1 -Live -SkipContact -CreatureFamily walking`, `scripts/build-corpse-catalogue.ps1 -Render` and `scripts/verify.ps1`.

Continue the configured occurrence queue with GiantSerpent21, then JackRabbit/Rabbit/SeaHorse/Wisp and every remaining family. Giant-serpent meshes and silver-donor motions have been audited but not accepted; their UV layouts differ, so mesh/UV equality is not claimed. No routine visual approval is required.
