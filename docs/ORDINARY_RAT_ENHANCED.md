# Ordinary Rat Enhanced actions

Body 238 uses the distributed small Rat by br-n518. The later Third Dawn model table also contains body 238. It names `Animals/rodents_rat_sewer_lod2.grn`, specifies scale 0.6 and links motion to body 215. The recovered original has 282 triangles and 34 joints. It uses the Giant Rat's atlas, UVs and skin weights, and the largest position difference from the Giant Rat mesh is below 0.000001 source units. The recovered body measures about 49 cm at its table scale. Its direct Enhanced frame members remain unlocated.

The optional presentation uses seven gestures from the linked Giant Rat motion set. The donor fits use 220 registered Enhanced frames from sequence `0x5ce4a899103b267`. The bake transfers pose deltas between the two rigs, combines the long tail chain into the target's two joints and scales travel to the small Rat's actual reach. All thirteen existing target clips and their keys are retained. The two ear pieces move closer to the skull on the fitted scene. The nearest ear and skull vertices measure 0.51 mm apart in the sampled neutral pose, down from 10.2 mm.

The normal client passed 11 Classic and 106 Enhanced checks. Finite terrain tests covered 1,890 skinned poses and 63 fall transfers. The largest nearest-paw gap was 3.31 mm and the largest joint displacement at handoff was below 0.11 micrometres. Forward+ rendered 24 views at the small body scale. A fresh bake reproduced the atlas, mesh, rig and all 540 animation tracks. Two isolated native clients passed 119 checks across the Rat and Sewer Rat constructors and both player sexes. The shared body preserves each constructor's own stats. The checks exercised stock taming through the real target packet, native weapon swing and hit reactions, movement, full and partial dyes, and the static corpse. The fixture sets the tamer's skill and sends the explicit Enhanced selectors.

Prepare the installed Giant Rat Enhanced scenes, then run `python -B scripts/prepare-ordinary-rat-enhanced.py --install` and start the client with `--enhanced-creature-actions`. The preparation validates the public Rat asset, the installed donor and the generated scene. The default client uses the [public CC0 presentation](RATS.md). Game data and the donor-derived scene remain local.
