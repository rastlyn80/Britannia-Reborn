# Water elemental: an independently distributable replacement

Body 16 now has an authored fluid-vortex candidate using our existing public Quaternius CC0 base and animation inputs. Its upper body retains the 65-bone male rig; a clipped waist joins an authored, tapered lower vortex. Eight skin influences retain all interpolated weights (measured discarded weight: zero). The 12,428-triangle animated surface has 80 unique waist intersections. A separate 80-triangle pool is visible at the end of death. This is a replacement interpretation, not a recovered Third Dawn water elemental.

The available Third Dawn model table names `elementals_water_elemental`, but the corresponding named mesh, rig and actions are absent from **this audited archive**. Three water mote/sparkle TGAs and nine structurally decoded particle files referencing a water mote exist. No actor binding or complete emitter behaviour has been established. These observations do not establish absence from every client version. Local source receipts: `work/creatures/water-occurrence-source.json` and `particle-source-audit.json`. No original water texture or particle data is included in the new public model.

The upper body/rig comes from [Quaternius Universal Base Characters Standard](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters); thirteen categories use retargeted clips from [Universal Animation Library Standard](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library). Both source packs have retained CC0 grants and archive/member hashes in `Viewer/Art/Quaternius`. Exact input pins and recipe: `scripts/prepare-water-elemental.py`, `PrepareWaterElemental.gd` and public `Viewer/Art/Creatures/Water/provenance.json`. The resulting authored adaptation, shader and recipe are GPL-3.0-or-later; underlying CC0 rights remain intact. There are no new downloads or proprietary inputs in the public bundle.

Fifteen categories cover Idle, Walk, Run, three attacks, two reactions, Fidget, three casting stages, Pillage and two death routes. Death/Death2 deliberately share **one** authored fluid collapse into a pool; they are not distinct recovered death directions. Walk/run use retargeted upper-body locomotion above an anchored vortex. Root translation is removed; every action explicitly restores living root scale and mesh visibility, so a death pose cannot leak into a later action. This is category coverage rather than original UO choreography. The catalogue's legacy `stats.original` key holds the replacement's declared clips for this independently licensed entry.

The procedural wet surface uses reflection, low roughness, clearcoat, normal ripples, foam and slight transparency. Its phase is an explicit `water_time` parameter rather than shader `TIME`. Raw server hue bits are retained: full hue recolours the surface, partial hue recolours authored moving foam zones. This is not a recovered original grey-pixel mask. The Godot 4.7 shader uses `CLEARCOAT_ROUGHNESS`; see the [official spatial shader reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.html). No texture-generation service or external image is needed.

Corpse graphic **0x2006 / amount 16** automatically enters the existing body-variant catalogue. The final pool has no skeleton or animation player. Its shader gets an independent snapshot of the exact water phase; advancing the former actor cannot animate the static corpse. Material overrides are detached before destroying temporary posed actors, avoiding headless-renderer callbacks against released materials. Other actors retain their existing surface interpretation. The independently prepared scene loads without original CC0 source imports or any local-art directory.
