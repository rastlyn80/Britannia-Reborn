# Northern animals and Snow Elemental

The Polar Bear (213), both White Wolf rolls (34 and 37), Walrus (221) and Snow Elemental (163) retain their locally prepared Third Dawn skins and source rigs. The registry checks the saved scene hashes before it selects the terrain renderers. The optional Polar Bear, Walrus and Snow Elemental actions still use their separately fitted archive sequences. The source game meshes, textures and captured references remain local.

The Polar Bear previously overlapped the 22 cm test tread by 341 mm and both White Wolf rolls by 221 mm. The four-foot renderer passed 6,750 gait samples in each profile and 3,416 Enhanced Polar Bear gesture samples. It passed 96 Classic and 104 Enhanced decoded falls. The highest tread still produces a body lift of about 110 mm for the Polar Bear and 90 mm for the wolves. The actual server constructors and native movement passed 64 Classic and 67 Enhanced checks. The tests applied a partial hue to the White Wolf and confirmed that both body rolls retain it in their static corpses. The human carver obtained 18 hides and two ribs from the bear, and seven hides and one rib from each wolf.

The Walrus keeps its sliding belly movement and original articulated flippers. Its old attack overlapped the test step by 276 mm. The new renderer passed 2,790 Classic and 4,340 Enhanced full-skin poses and 56 or 80 decoded falls. The highest tread still produces a visible 113 mm lift during the run. The two native cases passed 46 Classic and 52 Enhanced checks. The normal corpse preserved the stock or partial test hue, and each human carver obtained 14 hides and one rib. The stock definition is 12 hides before the native human bonus.

The Snow Elemental previously intersected the high tread by 220 mm. The fitted rise now holds the centre tread as the silhouette grows. The renderer supports fitted stances without trying to move their feet to standing gait targets. The measured torso lift during the shoulder alert fell from 962 mm in the initial test to 14 mm. The packed rise still lifts visibly by up to 137 mm between samples on the highest tread. Both profiles passed the clearance replays (3,100 Classic and 3,720 Enhanced full-skin poses) and the packet-driven falls (56 and 80). The 44 Classic and 50 Enhanced native checks cover real movement, controlled action packets, partial hue, the full-scale recovery when movement interrupts the rise, and a frozen corpse retaining the three packed iron ore and black pearls.

Native test cases use the genuine ModernUO constructors, Move, Kill and corpse-carve methods in an isolated Trammel world. Visual deaths become body variants of native graphic 0x2006. Run scripts/verify.ps1 with the locally prepared art to repeat the terrain and packet replays. The dedicated tests are named test-source-arctic, test-source-walrus and test-source-snow in the scripts directory.
