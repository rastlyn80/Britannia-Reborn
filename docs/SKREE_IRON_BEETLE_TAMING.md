# Skree and Iron Beetle taming

Two disposable players used the actual Animal Taming skill and targeting packets against creatures obtained from native spawners configured with the Iron Beetle and Skree names. The trial placed them on Ter Mur and held them in range. Each player had 120 Animal Taming and Animal Lore.

The healthy Iron Beetle refused taming with cliloc 1054025. After the fixture reduced its health below ten percent, the same player tamed it and occupied four follower slots. The native taming handler reduced its maximum health from 803 to 401. The second player saw the Skree's anger response, cliloc 502805, and tamed it through a subsequent attempt. The Skree occupied four follower slots and retained its Mystic AI. Both creatures retained their serials and owners after a server restart.

The fixture applied lethal damage to a separate group of wild Iron Beetles. One of 16 corpses contained an Undamaged Iron Beetle Scale with graphic 0x5742 and label 1112905. This verifies the loot path. The trial passed 29 initial and 10 restart checks.

Thepem's Metal Head and Zosilem's Armor Up each require ten scales. A separate native quest trial verified both turn-ins. [Alchemist trials](ALCHEMIST_CREATURE_TURNINS.md)
