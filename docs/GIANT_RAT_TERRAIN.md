# Giant Rat terrain, taming and corpse

The Giant Rat keeps body 215 and its own recovered Third Dawn rig and material. Ordinary Rat and Sewer Rat continue to use their shared body 238. The seven optional Enhanced motions retain their fitted source keys.

The terrain replay sampled flat ground, a slope and a 22 cm tread. The candidate kept at least 2 mm of clearance in all 1,860 Classic and 4,030 Enhanced complete-skin poses. The original renderer fell below that target in 1,855 and 2,669 samples respectively. The highest tread can still visibly lift the mesh, by about 191 mm between samples during Tail Unfurl.

The decoded death replay passed 64 Classic and 120 Enhanced handoffs, including the two Classic death groups and interruptions of all seven Enhanced motions. It measured 1,088 and 2,040 fall poses. The first displayed pose shifted by at most 0.508 mm. The frozen body preserves its partial hue in native graphic `0x2006` and settles about 2 mm above the final surface.

The isolated server passed 51 Classic and 65 Enhanced checks across both test owners in each profile. It verified the native constructor stat ranges, hues, walk and run, stock swing and hit packets and the static corpse. Both owners tamed a wild Giant Rat through the real skill target and received its follower slot. Native human carving yielded seven hides and one rib. The Enhanced selectors were controlled test packets.

The source provenance is recorded in the existing creature and Enhanced-action manifests. The local reproduction scripts, terrain measurements and native receipts are under `work/creatures/native-form-audit/enhanced-source-gaps/common-forms-enhanced/giant-rat-living-source/`.
