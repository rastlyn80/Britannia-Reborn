# Evil Mage terrain and Lightning

Body 124 keeps its original Third Dawn rig and independent body and robe colour routes. The optional Manifest and Crossed Ward retain the fitted source keys. Native movement can interrupt Manifest and restore full scale. The Evil Mage Lord's other bodies remain on their existing routes.

The terrain replay measured 3,100 Classic and 3,720 Enhanced complete-skin poses. The baseline fell below the target in 2,510 and 2,649 samples respectively. The updated renderer clears the target in every sampled pose. Using a common starting height for both feet reduced the largest sampled step correction in the trial from about 418 mm to 220 mm. The high tread still produces visible lift in some poses.

The death replay passed 56 Classic and 72 Enhanced decoded handoffs with 952 and 1,224 complete-skin samples. The largest first-pose shift was about 0.509 mm. The static corpse preserved its body and hue with final clearance of about 2 mm.

The isolated server passed 34 Classic and 40 Enhanced checks across both stock owner cases in each mode. The native robe reached the material renderer, the stock Lightning targeting cursor dealt real damage to a disposable Ogre, and both clients received movement and matching corpses. The Enhanced run verified the compact Manifest and full-scale recovery after a native run. Spell choice and the Enhanced selectors were controlled by the fixture.
