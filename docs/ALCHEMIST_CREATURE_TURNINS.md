# Thepem and Zosilem creature turn-ins

The native quest system now offers Thepem's All That Glitters, Tasty Treats, Metal Head and Pink is the New Black, and Zosilem's Dabbling on the Dark Side and Armor Up. The first five completed apprentice quests unlock Thepem's specialist tier. Five specialist completions unlock Zosilem, and ten of her first-tier completions unlock Armor Up. First-tier quests have a 30-minute repeat delay. Second-tier quests have a two-hour repeat delay. Completion counts and the built-in quest cooldowns persist.

Metal Head uses ten Undamaged Iron Beetle Scales and one inspected pile each of Dull Copper, Shadow Iron, Copper and Bronze. Copper inspection now converts a pile of twenty Copper Ingots into the matching native-label item. The reward converts up to 500 Iron Ingots into one of the four lesser metals. Armor Up uses five Boura Skins, ten Leather Wolf Skins and ten Undamaged Iron Beetle Scales. Its reward grants an owned pet +10 physical resistance for ten minutes, followed by two hours of recovery. Physical resistance uses ModernUO's native modifier as an approximation of the pinned reference's temporary damage absorption.

The isolated trial passed 139 initial and 10 restart checks. The normal client accepted both scale quests, marked their exact ingredients through the player context menu, turned them in and claimed both rewards. The fixture supplied ingredients and seeded prerequisite completion history. Metal conversion preserved fifty excess Iron Ingots from a stack of 550. The Armor Essence was targeted through the normal client at the player's owned Iron Beetle, and its +10 effect persisted across restart. The two completed quests remained on cooldown after restart. The other four quest turn-ins and reward effects still need their own trials. Zosilem's other first-, second- and third-tier quests and the alchemy bulk orders remain open.
