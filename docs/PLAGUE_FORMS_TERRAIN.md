# Earth Elemental and Slime Plague Spawn forms

The native Plague Spawn still chooses its own body and hue. Earth Elemental body 14 and Slime body 51 keep their distinct Third Dawn rigs, materials and motions. Headless One, Giant Serpent, Gorilla and both human forms use their existing renderers. The new support is client-side and follows the server's native body and position.

Earth Elemental passed 4,030 Classic and 5,270 Enhanced full-skin terrain samples. The Classic assembly starts below the ground as authored, and the final pose clears the tread. The optional Enhanced assembly retains the visible pieces and uses the floor under each fragment when it crosses a ledge. Eighteen interruptions of that assembly on a 22 cm tread produced 558 sampled fall poses. The minimum clearance was 2.00 mm and the largest entry displacement was 0.12 micrometres. The frozen corpse retained the visible stones and server hue. Ordinary body falls passed 56 Classic and 80 Enhanced decoded handoffs.

The legless Slime keeps its source bounce and gel surface. Its former Fidget intersected the 22 cm tread by 222 mm. The new support passed 2,170 Classic and 2,790 Enhanced full-skin terrain samples, plus 56 and 72 decoded corpse handoffs. Across both bodies the largest ordinary first-frame corpse displacement was 0.51 mm. The highest test tread still causes a visible lift of up to 237 mm for the Earth Elemental and 219 mm for the Slime during particular motions.

The native owned-spawn test passed 328 checks per client profile. Both isolated clients observed all seven real body rolls, with two owners per roll, through the stock Plague Beast melee callback. The native server removed owned spawns after owner range, map change, deletion and unmodified one-minute expiry. Those lifecycle removals produced no corpse. This test exercises the actual owner relationship and body and hue replication. The terrain and corpse checks use separate decoded packet replays.

Original source and generated scene provenance remain in [Original Slime and PlagueBeast](AMORPHOUS_CREATURES.md) and the existing Earth Elemental catalogue. Source art and native test accounts stay outside Git.
