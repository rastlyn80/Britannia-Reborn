# Stygian Dragon

Stygian Dragon uses body 826 and static corpse graphic `0x2006`. Third Dawn assigns that body number to an Ostard. The replacement retains the Third Dawn red dragon body 172 skeleton and skin weights. Its 57 joints drive folded wings, an extended tail, two melee strikes, a defensive wing motion, a takeoff, a breath windup and two falls. The surface uses slate colouring, red membranes and brass-toned horn details. The fit reuses the donor's flight cycle and landing.

Enhanced 4.0.117.33 supplied 1,360 decoded AMOU frames in 20 indexed groups from `AnimationFrame2.uop`. The input manifest pins the individual URL and hash for each group and all three donor scenes. Five original dragons were compared as donors. The original treatment uses 1,350 skinned vertices. The rounded and modern treatments use 21,600 each. The expanded game files and baked assets stay local. [Enhanced source audit](UNDERWORLD_SENTINEL_AND_ABYSS_REFERENCES.md)

Prepare the normal body-172 assets. Put the expanded members in a local directory under the names `ec-frame-826-<group>.bin`, then run `python -B scripts/prepare-stygian-dragon.py --reference <directory>`. It validates the inputs and installs the three scenes and their index under `Viewer/local-art/creatures/stygian-dragon`. The optional profile is checked by `scripts/verify.ps1` when prepared.

The stock ModernUO Ter Mur spawner at (326, 159, 20) produced body 826 within the configured ten-tile home range on an isolated server. The native class uses the documented body, combat statistics, fire breath, melee abilities and basic corpse resources. A separate arena trial passed 14 checks per client profile. The fixture invoked the native fire breath and inserted a competing melee animation during its windup. Both clients launched from the posed muzzle, observed server damage and froze the dyed body on the static corpse. Measured launch delays were 1.264 seconds in Classic and 1.259 seconds in Enhanced. The trial exercised the ability and its native timers. Autonomous selection and boss-specific attacks remain open.

World replays passed 50 Classic and 60 Enhanced checks in both headless and Forward+ runs. They covered the fitted groups, both falls and both corpse and death packet orders. The static corpse remained dyed and used the selected fall without a runtime skeleton. Terrain samples checked 2,106 grounded poses on flat ground, a slope and a 22 cm step. Minimum clearance was 2.5 mm. The largest upward correction was 31.2 cm while running onto the step. Idle, run and both fall endpoints were also inspected on the Forward+ renderer.

The meteor attack, fire line, stygian fireball, boss head and encounter rewards remain open. The 3D fit still needs a closer pass on the horns and armour and finer matching for flight, landing and subtle idle variants.
