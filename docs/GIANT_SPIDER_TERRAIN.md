# Giant Spider footing and corpse transitions

ModernUO's stock Giant Spider uses body 28. The retained Third Dawn model is `Monsters/arachnids_spider_giant_lod2.grn`. It has 187 source points, 246 triangles, 30 joints and the original `arachnids_spider_giant_v01.tga` atlas. The original mesh has SHA-256 `cdded0b16f60899138e219e1e34425e3e6d8602cf53be286a1793bd5342a6503`. Its recovered motions include the walk, run, idle, attack, hit, fidget and death. Both client profiles retain the established Classic actions. The cached Enhanced 4.0.117.33 sequence, definitions and indexed patch manifests contain no direct body 28 match. Unidentified aliases and the loose directory are open.

The eight articulated legs now follow the visual ground. The source Walk and Run translate the whole spider over a stride. The renderer removes the net horizontal travel so the server position can move the creature, preserves the vertical motion, and blends the loop ends. Across the 22 cm tread the belly begins to rise in advance and the lower legs can reach down. The client retains the original strike and both collapse routes.

The unmodified body clipped by up to 45 cm on the test slope. The early footing prototype cleared the slope but could shift the model abruptly by 22 cm at a ledge. The final movement held roughly 3 mm of minimum skin clearance across 13,500 sampled poses. It cut the largest flat-ground loop jump from 1.43 m to 4 cm and the ledge's largest per-sample body lift to 25 mm on the modern mesh and 36 mm on the original mesh. Fast limb movements and up to 79 mm of clearance under the nearest visible vertex remain on the high step.

The client passed 144 decoded transfers and 2,448 sampled collapse poses across the original, rounded and modern meshes. The collapse starts from the displayed pose, continues an already playing alternate fall and freezes body 28 with its native hue. The first measured pose changed by less than 4 micrometres.

The isolated ModernUO trial covers the stock constructor, Animal Taming, Arachnid pack instinct, one follower slot, stock walking and running, dye and the visible static corpse. The source declares Regular poison on hit. This terrain trial leaves combat and poison to the existing server implementation.

Reproduce with Godot headless, `--path Viewer --script res://../scripts/test-giant-spider-living-crossing.gd` and `res://../scripts/test-giant-spider-living-fall.gd`. Add `-- --enhanced-creature-actions` for the optional profile. The crossing test also accepts `-- --baseline` for the existing source renderer. Build the local action scenes with `scripts/bake-giant-spider-actions.gd` and validate them with `scripts/prepare-spider-mongbat-actions.py`. Original game files and derived local scenes remain outside Git.
