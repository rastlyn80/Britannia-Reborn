# Red and Black Solen Warriors on slopes and steps

The Red Solen uses body 782. The Black Solen uses body 806 and retains its native 0x453 hue. The two warriors and their infiltrator variants keep the original six-legged rigs, eight-weight skin treatment and native identities. The Red warrior retains its Classic actions. Enhanced body 806 also retains its fitted gestures and the quiet idle selected by the server's combat flags.

The replay used flat ground, slopes and a 22 cm tread. The Enhanced living count includes 310 poses for the Black Solen quiet idle. Both falls retained the displayed starting pose within one micrometre and settled about 2 mm above the support. The Black Solen's third Classic attack can still lift abruptly on the highest tread. The largest sampled adjacent correction was about 312 mm. The fitted quiet idle's largest adjacent correction was about 2.5 mm.

The isolated server exercised all four stock classes with each of two disposable characters. Both connections received native walk, run and death. The normal client displayed the matching body, hue and static corpse. The Warriors left four stock acid pools each. Infiltrators left none. Each corpse held the stock roll of three or thirteen Zoogi Fungus. In the Enhanced tests the Black Warrior and Black Infiltrator changed between the combat idle and fitted quiet idle when the server changed their combat flag. The fixture controlled movement, hues, combat flags and gesture packets.
