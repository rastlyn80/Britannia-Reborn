## Motion

[KayKit Character Animations](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations), Kay Lousberg / KayKit, Free 1.1, **CC0-1.0**. Exact free upload ID 15799903. Archive SHA-256: `65882f31f905ad2e953819648a59287cdeab8f623908d5ef701971d3758be20f`. Only static GLB/data/text members were extracted; no bundled executable or Blender script was run. CombatRanged and General file hashes are in `scripts/human-coverage-inputs.json`. Ranged upper-body timing is retargeted and arm lengths corrected to shared grip targets. Turn, salute, third fidget and human rider poses are authored by this project. Existing Quaternius CC0 locomotion and its 45-clip foundation are retained; see `scripts/human-motion-inputs.json`.

## Body, fitted armour and authored parts

The accepted MakeHuman/CharacterCreator body is CC0 art from [the pinned CharacterCreator revision](https://github.com/kblood/CharacterCreator/tree/852e48f316b90a0937234886630e4fb4cdfd1f78); exact source downloads are pinned by `scripts/download-free-human.py`. The current body GLB hashes and source recipe chain are in the coverage input manifest. The rejected body-surface armour shells have been replaced. Continuous clothing backing derives from the pinned CC0 Toigo shirt, suits02 trousers and Viking tunic/boots foundation; its clothing topology is retained and fitted to body measurements. Outer cuirasses, articulated limb plates, shoulder domes, joint cups, overlapping waist lames and rivets are original procedural geometry with thickness and beveled edges. They do not copy body triangles or body textures. Jewellery, bows and the two new hat silhouettes are authored by Britannia Reborn. Interlocked mail-ring and brushed steel textures are generated by `scripts/human-armour.py`. All parts retain the original 61-joint skeleton. Exact fitted foundation hashes, including ankle-boot inputs, are in `scripts/human-coverage-inputs.json`; original source chains remain in `scripts/download-free-equipment.py`, `scripts/character-expansion-inputs.json` and HUMAN_CIVILIAN_BATCH.md.

Linen/fabric inputs are the existing traced CC0 MedievalKit materials (`Viewer/Art/MedievalKit/NOTICE.md` and `CREDITS.md`). Leather001 is [ambientCG Leather001](https://ambientcg.com/a/Leather001), CC0. The exact texture/archive/member hashes and download URL are in `material_provenance`. Existing body skins, eye materials, footwear and hair keep their prior provenance.
