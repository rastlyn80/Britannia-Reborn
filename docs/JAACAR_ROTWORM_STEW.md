# Jaacar and Rotworm Stew

The native Underworld spawner at Ter Mur (1204, 1041, −42) resolves Jaacar as body 723. Bad Company counts ten ordinary Green Goblins in the Underworld. Scouts and alchemists yield no credit. The reward box contains the native Cooking recipe scroll 500. The linked A Tangled Web quest counts twelve Bloodworms or Blood Elementals in the Underworld.
