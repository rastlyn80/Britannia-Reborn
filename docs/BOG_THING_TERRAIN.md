# Bog Thing and Tangle on slopes and steps

Bog Thing and Tangle share original body 780 and keep their separate stock colours and stats. The Third Dawn rig, fixed source motion clocks and Enhanced growth and tongue remain in use. The terrain treatment keeps the 103-point footprint and checks the full body on solid support. Both stock Classic death groups retain the original fall. Static corpse graphic 0x2006 preserves body, hue and the displayed mesh while the temporary tongue is hidden.
