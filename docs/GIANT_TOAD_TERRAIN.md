# Giant Toad terrain and taming

Body 80 uses its own Third Dawn toad rig and retains its hop, tongue, throat and fall motions. It shares the terrain handling used by BullFrog. The two fitted Enhanced gestures still play on the original Giant Toad skin. The fall freezes into static corpse graphic 0x2006 with the matching body and hue.

The largest adjacent living correction was about 223 mm during Spring Lunge on the highest tread. The first fall pose differed by at most 0.51 mm and the frozen mesh settled about 2 mm above the sampled support.

Two disposable players tamed wild Toads through the stock skill and target packets. Each Toad accepted raw meat, preserved its body and applied hues through death, and yielded 14 spined hides with the human carving bonus. Both clients received the stock unarmed swing. The fixture invoked the pinned swing and hurt methods, adjusted the disposable players' taming skill, and sent explicit animation packets for the gesture checks.
