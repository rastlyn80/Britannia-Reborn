# Maddening Horror

Body 721 uses the original Third Dawn lich body 24 as its robed donor. The model retains the original UV layout and the skeleton under the robe. It adds a pale head and 13 facial and lower-body tendrils with four joints each. The 88-joint rig drives gait, two melee attacks, mana drain, recoil and two falls. The modern and rounded scenes have 25,364 skinned vertices. The bronze-red robe, bone face and green tendrils retain distinct colours until a native hue applies.

Enhanced 4.0.117.33 supplied 785 decoded AMOU frames across 17 groups in `AnimationFrame2.uop`. The input manifest pins every member URL and hash and all three original donor scene hashes. No usable body-721 creature row was found in the checked Third Dawn tables. See the [Abyss reference audit](UNDERWORLD_SENTINEL_AND_ABYSS_REFERENCES.md).

Prepare the normal body-24 assets and expand the Enhanced members into a local directory as `ec-frame-721-<group>.bin`. Run `python -B scripts/prepare-maddening-horror.py --reference <directory>`. It validates the reference files and installs three content-addressed scenes under `Viewer/local-art/creatures/maddening-horror`. The official source and resulting local scenes remain outside Git. Run `scripts/verify.ps1` to check the prepared route.

The shipped ModernUO Abyss spawner at Ter Mur (745, 739, 16) produced body 721 within its five-tile home range. The spawn has 660 health and uses the checked sound IDs. A direct melee callback transferred mana from a nearby target and set the 30-second cooldown. The test also validated Vile Tentacles as commodity graphic `0x5727`, label 1113333. The 20% corpse-loot roll and the 10% mana-drain chance were inspected in source. Their rates were outside the live trial.

Both isolated client profiles passed 12 checks. The callback sent the mana loss to the client, played the fitted cast and effect, and preserved dye 1141 on the visible static corpse. The fixture invoked the melee callback, set the initial mana, and killed the creature through its native death method. Separate World replays passed 41 Classic and 49 Enhanced checks in headless and Forward+ runs. They exercised both corpse and death packet orders and retained the fall, hue and all three surfaces through the static handoff. Terrain checks sampled 2,574 poses across flats, slopes and a 22 cm step. Minimum clearance was 2.5 mm and the largest upward correction was 24.5 cm while running on the step.

Native AI uses ModernUO's mage combat. Necromancer spell selection and autonomous mana-drain timing still need verification. Several source idle and guard groups share a fitted movement. The model would benefit from closer facial anatomy and longer individual tentacle poses.
