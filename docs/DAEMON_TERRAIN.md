# Daemon on slopes and steps

Native Daemon body 9 retains the recovered Third Dawn rig, wings and material. The terrain profile checks the saved original, rounded and modern scene hashes. Both client profiles use the original Classic motions and the optional profile retains the four fitted Enhanced gestures.

The baseline overlapped the sampled slope by 475 mm during the attack and the high tread by 240 mm during landing. The new renderer uses the original wing joints to clear the terrain and a continuous descent for landing. The tests passed 5,270 Classic and 6,510 Enhanced full-skin poses. The highest tested tread still produces up to 169 mm of lift between takeoff samples and 214 mm during the second attack. A completed flight loop holds steady altitude on each sampled surface.

Decoded packet replays passed 64 Classic and 80 Enhanced falls, including takeoff, flight, landing and the alternate airborne collapse. The largest first-pose change was under 0.004 mm. The static corpse retains body 9 and the server hue under native graphic 0x2006.

Two isolated live connections passed 48 Classic and 54 Enhanced checks. Stock movement and Kill use the real class. The fixture sends the gesture packets for action selection and chooses the target for the stock LightningSpell. Both connections receive the actual lightning effect and the target loses health. Automatic AI spell choice is outside the fixture.
