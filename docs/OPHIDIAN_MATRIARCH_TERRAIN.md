# Ophidian Matriarch on slopes and steps

The stock Ophidian Matriarch retains body 87, the Third Dawn queen rig and separate Classic death animations. In Enhanced mode, the ceremonial sceptre remains attached to the original right-hand grasp during its five fitted actions. Its shaft uses the existing fitted art and material. The terrain support measures the whole body and includes the visible sceptre. An armed fall keeps the displayed prop on the frozen corpse. Classic and unarmed Enhanced corpses keep the original unarmed appearance.

The tests used flat ground, slopes and a 22 cm tread. They covered both Classic fall groups and interruption of every fitted Enhanced action, with the sceptre carried into the visible static corpse. The first joint shift stayed under two micrometres and the frozen body and prop cleared the support by about 2 mm. The largest adjacent living correction was about 91 mm during the walk on the highest tread.

The isolated server used the stock Matriarch with both disposable characters. It verified greater poison immunity, hostility to the Terathan Warrior and the level-four treasure map definition. The stock Lightning spell cursor damaged a disposable Ogre. Both clients received movement and death. The normal client displayed the server's body and hue and kept the ceremonial sceptre in the corpse after a controlled Enhanced gesture. Autonomous casting and treasure drops were outside these tests.

The existing asset provenance remains in the Third Dawn and Enhanced source records. The local verification and staged source are under work/creatures/native-form-audit/enhanced-source-gaps/common-forms-enhanced/ophidian-matriarch-living-source.
