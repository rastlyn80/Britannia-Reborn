# Silver Serpent Enhanced actions

The optional Enhanced profile adds nine motions to the original Silver Serpent body 92, with its own skin weights, UVs, atlas and material. The Third Dawn body table links Silver Serpent animation to Giant Serpent body 21. The inspected indexed and loose Enhanced archives supplied no direct body-92 frames. The motion references come from the [body-21 source ledger](../scripts/giant-serpent-enhanced-sequences.json).

The original meshes have matching vertex positions, triangles and weights. The thirteen weighted joints share their parent links and have no measured rest-translation difference. Their largest rest rotation difference is below 0.04 degrees. The two models use different texture layouts. The bake copies motion as rest-space deltas, skips the unweighted donor name joint and preserves the Silver Serpent's existing tracks. The added clips include combat slither, quiet idle, uncoiling strike, low-neck recoil, surfacing, three alert gestures and the alternate collapse. Classic collapse requests still use the existing fall.

Prepare the three base Silver Serpent treatments and the [Giant Serpent Enhanced scenes](GIANT_SERPENT_ENHANCED.md). Run `python -B scripts/prepare-silver-serpent-enhanced.py --install` and launch with `--enhanced-creature-actions`. The installer validates the donor manifest and all three scene hashes. A separate bake reproduced the atlas, geometry, skeleton and 1,419 animation tracks across the three treatments. Private source and baked art stay local.

The normal client passed 13 Classic and 117 Enhanced checks. Finite terrain tests covered 7,803 fully skinned poses and 108 fall transfers, including death while surfacing. Maximum sampled displacement at the transfer was 1.25 micrometres. Forward+ rendered 45 views with the native undyed colour. Two disposable native clients passed 47 stock constructor, movement, weapon hit, hue, death and corpse checks. The fixture sent the explicit Enhanced selectors. A separate 56-check trial on the same isolated server used real charm requests and targets. It exercised native familiar and target refusals, assignment of the selected combatant, the native melee callback for lethal poison and the serpent's lethal poison immunity.
